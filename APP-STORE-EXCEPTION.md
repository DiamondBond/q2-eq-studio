# App store exception

EQ Studio's licence is the GNU General Public License, version 3 (`LICENSE`). This file adds a permission to it, as section 7 of that licence allows ("additional permissions").

## Why

Apple's App Store and TestFlight, and stores like them, hand an app out under terms of their own: who may install it, on how many devices, and with what copy protection. The GPL lets nobody add restrictions to the rights it gives (section 10), so without this permission an app under it could not be offered there.

## The permission

You may convey EQ Studio, or a work based on it such as a port to another platform, in object code form through Apple's App Store and TestFlight, or through another app store whose terms or technical measures this licence would otherwise not allow, and the store may apply those terms to the copies it hands out, if both of these hold:

1. You comply with this licence in every other way.
2. The complete Corresponding Source of what you convey is there for anyone to take under this licence, at no charge, from a public network server that the app or its store page names (section 6d).

The store's terms reach only the copy a person gets from the store. Everyone keeps every right this licence gives over the source, and over anything built from it.
